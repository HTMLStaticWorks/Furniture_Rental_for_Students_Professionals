Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting with RentNest.

General Information

What is RentNest?
RentNest is a premium furniture rental service designed for students and professionals. We offer flexible terms, free delivery, and high-quality furniture on demand.
Where do you operate?
We currently operate in New York, Boston, Austin, and San Francisco, with plans to expand to more cities soon.

Orders & Delivery

How fast can I get my furniture?
We typically deliver within 48 to 72 hours of your order placement, depending on availability in your area.
Do you assemble the furniture?
Yes, our white-glove delivery service includes full assembly and placement in your room at no extra cost.

Payments

When am I billed?
Your first payment is due at checkout. Subsequent payments are billed automatically every 30 days from your delivery date.
Are there cancellation fees?
If you cancel before your term ends, an early termination fee equal to one month's rent applies.

Damages & Swaps

What if I stain the sofa?
Normal wear and tear is covered. For severe stains or structural damage, a repair or replacement fee may be charged to your card on file.
Can I swap items during my lease?
Yes! Long-term leases include free swaps. For short-term leases, a swap fee of $49 applies.
